Scout

Search video like Google to find the exact second someone says a name.

A desktop app that turns hours of stream or video footage into something you can search by name. Point it at a Twitch or YouTube VOD (or a local file), give it a name or phrase, and it downloads the audio, transcribes it with word level timestamps, and loosely matches every likely mention, including ones that are misspelled or phonetically close. Every hit becomes a short review clip with a timestamped link back to the original video.

21m 52s to scan 4 full VODs
~2 hrs manual equivalent (30 min/VOD)
~5.5× faster than doing it by hand
  • Verified on a batch of 4 VODs (6 to 10 hrs each): 183 timestamped mentions found in 21m52s total
  • Transcribes audio locally with faster-whisper, on CPU or GPU
  • Fuzzy name/phrase matching with an adjustable confidence threshold
  • Automatically cuts review clips around every hit, with adjustable padding
  • A full app around it: dashboard, scan library, saved searches, collections, and watch folders that automatically index new videos
  • Backed by a proper SQLite schema (sources, transcripts, jobs, search runs, matches), not flat files

Nexus

A local AI assistant inspired by JARVIS, with voice control and live system monitoring.

A desktop assistant that starts listening the moment Windows boots. No button to press. Say the wake word, and it transcribes your voice locally, reasons through a local LLM running entirely on the device, and answers back in natural, streamed speech, all while a live dashboard tracks CPU, GPU, and RAM in real time.

  • Wake word activation that runs continuously and starts automatically with Windows. Nothing to click, nothing to launch
  • Speech to text (faster-whisper) and LLM reasoning (Ollama) both run fully on the device; only voice output uses cloud text to speech for natural sound
  • Streams replies sentence by sentence into speech as they're generated, instead of waiting for the full response
  • Live CPU/GPU/RAM dashboard built with Tauri, reflecting the backend's real state
  • One shared conversation log across typed and spoken input alike
